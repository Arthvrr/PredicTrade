How it works

Linear Regression: A statistical method that models the relationship between a dependent variable (future price) and independent variables (historical prices, volume, etc.) by fitting a linear equation to observed data.

Simple Moving Average (SMA): An indicator that calculates the average of a selected range of closing prices, over a number of periods. It filters out the daily "noise" to reveal the underlying trend.

  • SMA 5: The average over the last 5 days (1 trading week). It reacts quickly to recent price changes.
  • SMA 10: The average over the last 10 days (2 trading weeks). It's slower and provides a broader view of the short-term direction.

Probable Range (RMSE): The Root Mean Squared Error measures the average distance between the model's past predictions and the actual prices. We use it to create a ± 1 Standard Deviation confidence bracket around tomorrow's prediction.
